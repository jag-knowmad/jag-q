AR Postcards

Your photographs, brought to life.

Print a photo, stick an NFC tag on the back, and hand it to someone. They tap their phone, point the camera at the picture, and the scene animates — right on top of the print. No app to install.

Your postcards

How it works

  1. Print the photo as a postcard.
  2. Program an NFC tag with the card's viewer link (each tile below has a “Copy link” button) and stick it on the back.
  3. Recipient taps the postcard with their phone — the link opens, the camera starts, and the animation plays over the photo.

Want to add your own photo? Open the Studio to generate a tracking marker in your browser, or run npm run add-card locally.

Built with MindAR + A-Frame. Works in mobile Safari & Chrome over HTTPS.
